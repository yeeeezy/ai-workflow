## Wall entry and WASD direction correction (2026-10-21)

The owner observed sideways tilt while first climbing a wall and occasional mismatch between WASD and perceived travel. Cause: procedural `MoveInput` combined the robot's actor forward/right axes, independent of the third-person camera; mouse orbit changed the view but not W/A/S/D movement. During a surface switch, `UpdateSurfaceAdhesion` projected the old forward vector onto the new support plane. That vector approaches zero when the robot faces a front wall, allowing an unstable heading and visible roll.

`GetProceduralMoveVector` now provides the input basis: on the floor, W/S follow the camera's horizontal heading projected onto the support plane and A/D use camera left/right; as a wall is entered, W/S blend toward the surface-projected world-up axis, giving climb up/down, while A/D stay lateral along the wall. Diagonal input is capped to unit length. `UpdateSurfaceAdhesion` aligns the robot's forward axis with the requested travel. Without travel, it transports the robot's right axis onto the new surface to preserve a stable no-roll orientation. This leaves the third-person camera independent while movement matches the view on the floor and remains predictable on a wall.
